The math

Three formulas, worked with the numbers on screen

Dot product
a · b  =  a₁b₁ + a₂b₂
= (4)(3) + (3)(4)
= 24
Vector norm
‖a‖  =  √(a₁² + a₂²)
= √(4² + 3²)
= 5.00
Cosine similarity
cos θ  =  (a · b) / (‖a‖‖b‖)
= 24 / (5.00 × 5.00)
= 0.96

Norm and dot product are the only two ingredients. Cosine similarity is just their ratio — it cancels out length and keeps only direction.
