What is an LLM?

It is not looking up an answer. It is picking the next chunk of text, then doing it again — a few hundred times a paragraph.

The problem it explains

There is no answer stored anywhere

People picture a giant filing cabinet being searched. The machine works the other way round: nothing is retrieved, everything is composed.

How it works

One choice, repeated

What “large” means

Hundreds of billions of dials

Those scores come from an enormous set of numbers — the parameters. Training nudges every one of them until the text it predicts starts to look like the text it was shown.

Nobody sets them by hand and nobody can read them. “Large” is a statement about how many there are, not about how much it knows.

So what

Fluent does not mean informed. The machine is optimised to sound like a good continuation — nothing in it checks whether that continuation is true.
