Training vs. using

Two completely different events get called “AI”. One took months and happened once. The other takes a second and happens every time you type.

TRAINING · once Months of compute. Enormously expensive. Everything it will ever know is fixed here.
USING · every chat Instant. Cheap. Changes nothing about the model.
↔

Drag the red line — that is the knowledge cutoff. Different models are trained at different times, so it sits in a different place for each one.

Asked about something before the line Answers from memory, instantly, and is usually right.
Asked about something after the line The dangerous case: it does not know that it does not know, so it often answers anyway.
