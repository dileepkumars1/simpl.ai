Why AI hallucinates

Not because it gets confused or unsure. Because nowhere in the machine is there a step that compares what it is about to say against the world.

Asked

What is Northwind Traders’ return policy?

Answered

Choosing the number in “returns within ___ days”

The myth this kills

It does not sound unsure, because it is not unsure

“30 days” is the most common return window on the internet, reported with the confidence that frequency earns. It answered the question it can answer — what does a return policy usually say — and you asked a different one.
