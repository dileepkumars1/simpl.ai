Open vs. closed models

One building lets you in the door. The other only opens a window — and which one you're using decides what you're actually allowed to do with it.

A heads-up for this module: pages 22–27 use some real industry terms (like "API," just below) you'll meet outside this course too — you don't need to memorize every one on first read.

Closed
You send a request through that window — usually called an API — and get an answer back. The weights stay inside; you never touch them.
Open
You take a copy of the actual numbers home — to run as-is, change, or put on your own machine.
