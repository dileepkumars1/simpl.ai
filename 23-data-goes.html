Durability note

The exact terms change. The questions don't.

Every vendor writes this differently, and rewrites it often — naming one here would be stale within months. Ask any vendor, for any tier, these three things instead:

1 — Retained?Is my input and output stored after the request finishes, or discarded?
2 — For how long?Days? Indefinitely? Can I set or shorten that window myself?
3 — Trained on?Does this tier feed back into training future models, by default or by choice?

The route you send a prompt through decides its whole afterlife — ask, don't assume.
