Attention, as a weighted lookup

Every word gets to ask a question — its Query. Every word offers an answer — its Key. Whichever Keys match best hand over their Value, blended by how well they matched.

Query — "it" Key — every word Value — what gets pulled in

How it works

One score per pair, then a blend

"Query," "Key," and "Value" are three different projections of the same word vector — three lenses on the same thing, not three separate pieces of data someone hands the model.

The scale of it

Every word does this, at the same time

"It" isn't special — every word in the sentence runs its own query against every other word's key, all in one pass. Darker squares mean a stronger match.

So what

The weights are not memorized associations — they're computed fresh from this sentence. Swap one word and the pull point lands somewhere else entirely.
