Why one head isn't enough

The lookup from page 33 is one head, asking one kind of question. Run the same word through four heads and each one comes back with a different answer.

Hand-authored to illustrate a well-documented pattern — real heads specialize this cleanly only sometimes, and never with labels attached.

How it works

Four lookups, glued back together

Real models run 8, 16, sometimes over 100 heads per layer, and stack many layers — this shows the shape of the mechanism with a number small enough to actually look at.

So what

One head has to compromise into a single blurry average. Several heads let the model hold several relationships at once and combine them later.
