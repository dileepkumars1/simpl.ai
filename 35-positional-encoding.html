How order survives without a scanning eye

Attention compares content against content — it never looks at where a word sits. Without help, "the dog bit the man" and "the man bit the dog" would look identical to it.

How it works

A position gets added, not looked up

Same word, two different slots → two different combined vectors. This is what makes "who did what" solvable at all. The real signal (from the original transformer paper) is a set of sine and cosine waves at different frequencies, smooth and unique per position — the bars above are a stand-in for the idea, not the actual formula.

So what

Without a position signal, every rearrangement of the same words would mean the same thing to the model. Position is what lets word order carry meaning at all.
