What fine-tuning actually changes

"Fine-tuning" can mean two very different things to the weights themselves — updating every one of them, or leaving them untouched and bolting on something small instead.

How it works

A small patch, added at the output

A and B are chosen small on purpose ("low-rank") — their product can only nudge the output in a limited number of directions, which is enough to teach a style or a skill without needing to re-derive everything the frozen weights already know.

So what

One frozen base model can carry dozens of different LoRA adapters — swapped in and out — instead of storing dozens of full-size fine-tuned copies.
