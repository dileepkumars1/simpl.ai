When a bigger context window replaces retrieval, and when it doesn't

The last two pages assumed you'd retrieve first. But a long-context model can just re-read everything, every turn, and skip retrieval entirely — step through corpus sizes and watch when that stops being the cheaper option.

How it works

Two answers to "what can the model see"

Neither one is "correct." Below the crossover, stuffing it all in is simpler and cheaper. Above it, retrieval is the only approach that still scales.

So what

A long context window doesn't replace retrieval — it just raises the corpus size where retrieval starts paying for itself.
