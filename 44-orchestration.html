One bottleneck, or none

Three specialist agents can be wired together two ways. Toggle between them and watch the same task travel a completely different path.

TASK: "Plan a 4-day trip to Lisbon under $1,200."

    How it works

    Same three specialists, two different wirings

    Neither shape is strictly better. A supervisor trades speed for oversight; peer-to-peer trades oversight for speed.

    So what

    A supervisor gives you one place to look when something breaks. Peer-to-peer gives you no bottleneck — pick the tradeoff you can afford to debug.
