A continuum, forced onto a short list of allowed values

Every value on the top line is real. Every value must round down to the nearest mark on the bottom line — that gap is the price of quantization.

So what

Every one of a model's billions of weights gets this exact same haircut — a little precision traded for a lot less memory.
