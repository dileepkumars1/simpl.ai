Learning how confident, not just what

A student model isn't just trained on the teacher's right answer — it's trained on the teacher's entire probability distribution. Toggle the student's training state and watch its shape converge.

How it works

Trained on the whole distribution, not the single label

Matching the shape of "how wrong" every incorrect option was turns out to carry a lot of the teacher's judgment — far more than the single right label alone.

So what

The student doesn't just learn the right answer — it learns how confident the teacher was about every wrong one too.
