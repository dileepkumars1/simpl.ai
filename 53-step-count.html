Many small guesses, not one big leap

Each denoising step only removes a little noise, then re-checks against the prompt. Skip steps, and the result gets rougher — it doesn't just arrive at the same picture faster. Same starting noise, same prompt, four different step budgets.

An illustrative simulation, not a running image model — but the shape of the tradeoff is real.

How it works

Fewer checks, rougher answer

So what

A "step" isn't overhead to skip — it's a chance to correct the picture, and fewer chances means a rougher result.
